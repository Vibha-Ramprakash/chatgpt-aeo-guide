The Interviewer

It questions you first, then finds the real questions your buyers ask ChatGPT and Google.

Claude or CodexDataForSEO MCP
Prompt 1 · Interviewer
You are my Interviewer. Before you research anything, interview me. Ask one question at a time, 8 questions max, and wait for my answer each time:
1. What I sell, and my best seller
2. Who buys it, and why they pick me over others
3. Where I sell and ship, and my price range
4. My website and my 3 biggest competitors

Then use the DataForSEO connector (Keyword Data + AI Optimization) to find the real questions people in my niche ask ChatGPT and Google.

Give me my top 10 buyer questions as a table: question, monthly searches, intent (buy, compare or learn), and whether my site answers it today.

Never guess a number. If DataForSEO has no data for a question, say so.
2

The Rewriter

The first two lines of a page are what AI actually quotes. This makes them answer your buyer questions.

Claude or CodexYour site or CMS · you approve
Prompt 2 · Rewriter
You are my Rewriter. Here are my 10 buyer questions: [paste the Interviewer's table].

Read every page on my website: [URL, site folder, or Shopify / WordPress export].

For every page:
1. Match it to the one buyer question it should answer.
2. Rewrite the first two lines so they answer that question directly, in plain words, with a real number: price, size range, delivery time.
3. Add an FAQ section with 3 to 5 of the buyer questions, answered in 2 to 3 sentences each.
4. Make it AI readable: one H1, the questions as H2s, short paragraphs, FAQPage schema.

Show me a before and after for every page. Change nothing until I approve.

Prompt 3b · Gatekeeper check
Run /seo geo on [https://mywebsite.com].

Then check my robots.txt, my sitemap, and any firewall or CDN bot settings (Cloudflare, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace) for these AI crawlers: OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended.

1. Tell me which ones are blocked, and where they're blocked.
2. Explain what each one does in one line. OAI-SearchBot is the one that puts me in ChatGPT search.
3. Give me the exact lines to fix it, plus where to click in Cloudflare if that's the blocker.
4. If I don't want my content used for AI training, keep GPTBot blocked but allow OAI-SearchBot.

Change nothing until I approve.

Products: the Feed Builder · Services: your listings

ChatGPT can take a product feed straight from you. Service businesses get found through Google and Bing listings.

Claude or CodexChatGPT merchant program · applyGoogle Business Profile · freeBing Places · free
Prompt 4a · Feed Builder (products)
You are my Feed Builder. Read my store: [Shopify / WooCommerce export, product CSV, or my product page URLs].

1. Build a product feed that follows OpenAI's Product Feed Spec (developers.openai.com/commerce). Every product needs all 9 required fields: item_id, title, description, url, brand, seller_name, image_url, availability, price.
2. Use group_id to group sizes and colours of the same product.
3. Flag every product that's missing a required field, and tell me where to find it.
4. Rewrite titles and descriptions the way a shopper would actually ask for them.

Give me the feed as a CSV, ready to submit once I'm approved at chatgpt.com/merchants.
Prompt 4b · Listings (services)
I run a service business: [what I do] in [my city]. My top buyer questions are: [paste them].

1. Give me a checklist to fill my Google Business Profile 100%: primary and secondary categories, every service with a price, hours, service area, 10+ photos, and products.
2. Write my business description (750 characters max) so it answers my top 3 buyer questions in plain words.
3. Write 5 Q&As for my profile, using my buyer questions.
4. Then walk me through Bing Places: import from Google in one click, then fix anything that didn't copy over.

My name, address and phone must match my website exactly, everywhere.
5

Real reviews

Good or bad. The more real, the better.

Prompt 5 · Reviews
Write me a short, warm message asking my recent customers for an honest review, good or bad, on Google and on my website.

1. Mention what they bought, and when it arrived.
2. Say why it matters: every review helps the next person decide, and tells us what to fix.
3. If I offer a thank-you discount, it goes to everyone who leaves a review, whatever the rating, and the message says so clearly.
4. Give me a WhatsApp version (under 60 words) and an email version with a subject line.

Sound like a real person, not a brand. The more real, the better.

The AEO Tracker, every Monday

Open the AEO Tracker (free, works in your browser), then set it to check your 10 questions every week.

Prompt 6 · Scheduled task
Set this up as a scheduled task that runs every Monday at 9am:

1. Ask my 10 buyer questions using the DataForSEO AI Optimization API (LLM Responses for ChatGPT): [paste my questions].
2. For each question, record whether [my brand] is recommended, and at what position.
3. Add this week's column to my AEO Tracker artifact and update the rank chart.
4. Mark the week ChatGPT first recommends me, and flag any week I drop off.
5. Tell me what changed since last week and the one fix most likely to move me up.

Draft the fix, but don't publish anything until I approve.
